About tbc

Teen Builders Club is a community of likeminded, curious, ambitious teen builders. It's for people who love tinkering, creating, and building — and who want to meet others who are just as driven. We host meetups , demo nights, and co-working sessions.


We also run an online space to share projects, get feedback, and find other builders to jam with. This is a place built by and for young people who just want to ship cool stuff together.
