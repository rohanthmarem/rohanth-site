Had a conversation with a younger peer from school. Honestly it was a good wake up call for myself, and below is the nicely formatted version of the conversation.

**Why didn't you start a biz if you don't mind me asking?**

Fair question. Combination of laziness + ambition and sunk cost—which is really just fear. I realized this end of grade 11, last summer. Most of my HS time was already gone, so I thought: just wait a couple more months, then I can do whatever without worrying about school. Seemed fine in my head.

Now that those months passed? I realize how much I've missed out on.

**What did they do?**

Friends who completely turned their lives around in 6 months. Out of HS, yeah, but still. YC admits. High five-figure internships at top tech companies for 4 months. Deals with VCs. One guy heading to Nvidia.

You can just make opportunities for yourself. I never took action on my ideas, never saw them through, assumed they'd eventually come to me. Gifted kid syndrome. A bit of ADHD. Mainly just me being lazy.

Lazy, here, obviously refers to fear disguised.

**Could we do something simultaneous to HS?**

Yeah, for sure. I kind of did with TensorForest—the drone project. Got $10K in funding, talks with VCs, momentum. But it died because taking it to the next level meant majorly sacrificing school. I made that choice. Cost was something I didn't want to accept.

**So what do we do now?**

Good question. Figuring that out myself at the moment, I'll let you know what I find.

But for you: you've got an endless supply of opportunity out there. One that rewards your curiosity, innovation, interests—rather than the ones imposed on you in school. Leverage that.

Don't drop out and raise $15M. That's stupid unless you've shown it's a banger idea. But also don't spend your year studying HL math, writing your EE, stressing over grades. Don't make that your main option.

**Even if it isn't a trillion dollar company...**

Precisely. Do something you're interested in. Grow as far as you want, any direction you want, without boundaries of societal expectation. Maybe someone else likes it and you'll be 🤑. Maybe you just grow, have some fun chasing dreams. Either way: don't be stagnant.
